Rules & Regulations

Safety First. Fair Racing. Respect Everyone.

General Rules

  • All participants must register before joining any karting session.
  • Arrive at least 30 minutes before the event.
  • Follow all instructions given by race officials.
  • Respect fellow drivers and club members.
  • No alcohol or drugs are allowed before racing.

Safety Rules

  • Wear a helmet at all times.
  • Wear closed-toe shoes.
  • Fasten your seat belt before driving.
  • Long hair must be tied up.
  • Report damaged equipment immediately.

Racing Rules

  • Drive only in the correct direction.
  • No dangerous driving.
  • No intentional bumping.
  • Overtake only when safe.
  • Follow all marshal instructions.
