Required Attire and Equipment

For the safety of all members, participants must wear appropriate clothing and use the required safety equipment before entering the karting track.

Item Requirement Description
🪖 Helmet Required A certified helmet must be worn at all times while driving. Helmets are usually provided by the karting track.
👕 Comfortable Shirt Required Wear a comfortable T-shirt or long-sleeved shirt that allows free movement.
👖 Long Pants Required Long pants help protect the legs from heat and minor scratches.
👟 Closed-Toe Shoes Required Sports shoes or sneakers must be worn. Slippers and sandals are not allowed.
🧤 Racing Gloves Recommended Improve steering wheel grip and provide extra comfort while driving.
🏁 Racing Suit Competition Only Required during official karting competitions for additional protection.

Safety Reminder

  • Always fasten your helmet securely before entering the track.
  • Follow all instructions given by race marshals and instructors.
  • Do not wear loose clothing, scarves, or dangling accessories.
  • Tie up long hair before putting on the helmet.
  • Report any damaged equipment before the race begins.
