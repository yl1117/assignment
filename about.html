About APU Go Karting Club

Driven by Passion, Powered by Teamwork

About Us

  • The Apu Go-Karting Club is a student club that brings together students who are passionate about go kart racing and motorsports.

  • We welcome all the students of all skill levels, whether they are beginners or experienced drivers.

  • Our club provide a friendly environent where members can learn, improve and enjoy the excitement of karting together.



Our Mission

  • Our mission is to promote interest in go kart racing and motorsports among APU students.

  • We provide opportunities for members to enhance their driving skills.

  • We also organise fun, safe and exciting karting activities.

  • We are going to encourage teamwork, friendship and sportsmanship.



Our Vision

  • Our vision is to become one of the most active and inspiring student clubs at APU by creating a community where everyone can enjoy karting, develop their skills and build lasting friendships.



Objectives

Driving Skills

Help members improve their driving skills and confidence.

Teamwork

Encourage teamwork, sportsmanship and friendship among members.

Events

Organise fun, safe and exciting karting events and competitions.

Friendship

Build lasting friendships through club activities and events.



Club History

  • The Apu Go-Karting Club was established to bring together studenst who share an interest in go kart racing and motorsports.

  • The club welcomes students of all experience levels and provides opportunities to learn, improve and enjoy karting through various activities and events.
